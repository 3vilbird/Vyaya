Privacy Policy for Vyaya (व्यय)


This Privacy Policy explains how Vyaya (“the App”) handles information when you use it on your Android device.

Vyaya is a local-first expense tracker. It is designed to store your expense records on your device and does not operate a backend server, user-account system, analytics service, advertising service, or remote data-sync service.
Information We Collect

Vyaya does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data to us or any third party.

The App stores the following information only locally on your Android device:

    Expense amount, currency, date, and time

    Merchant or description text

    Expense category

    Payment method label, such as Cash, UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other

    Optional notes, UPI ID, and payment reference details that you choose to enter

    Expense status, such as Recorded, Paid, Pending, Failed, Cancelled, or Unknown

    Locally created categories and app preferences

    Locally generated CSV or Excel export files
    

This information is stored in an on-device SQLite database within the App’s private storage. It is not uploaded to any server controlled by the developer.
Information We Do Not Collect

Vyaya does not collect or transmit:


    Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address

    Account credentials or passwords

    UPI PINs, CVV numbers, full card numbers, or banking login credentials

    Bank account balances or transaction data from banking apps

    Precise or approximate location data

    Contacts, photos, microphone audio, or camera data

    Advertising identifiers

    Device identifiers for tracking purposes

    Usage analytics, crash telemetry, or behavioral profiling data
    

Vyaya does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking libraries.
How Vyaya Uses Information

All information you enter is used solely to provide the App’s core features:

    Recording and displaying expenses

    Calculating daily, monthly, and category-based spending summaries

    Displaying expense history and digital receipt details

    Enabling offline search, filtering, and reporting

    Generating local CSV or Excel export files when you request them

Vyaya does not use your data for advertising, profiling, machine-learning training, or sharing with data brokers.
Data Storage and Security

Your expense data remains stored locally in the App’s private application storage on your device. Vyaya does not send this data over the internet to the developer or any third-party server.

You are responsible for the security of your device, Android lock-screen protection, backups, and any exported files you choose to share. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, locally stored expense records may be permanently deleted.
Data Sharing

Vyaya does not share your personal or expense data with the developer, advertisers, analytics providers, or any other third parties.

If you use Android’s share sheet to export or send a CSV/Excel file, the selected destination—such as email, WhatsApp, Google Drive, or your device Files app—will receive the file you explicitly choose to share. Vyaya does not choose, monitor, or control that destination.
Permissions

Vyaya is designed to use minimal Android permissions. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, call logs, SMS messages, or financial accounts.

If a future version requests an Android permission, it will only be used for a clearly described App feature, and this Policy will be updated accordingly.
Children’s Privacy

Vyaya is not directed at children under 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it does not collect personal information from any user because all data remains local to the user’s device.
Data Retention and Deletion

Your expense records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App’s data, or uninstall Vyaya. The developer has no ability to access, recover, back up, or delete your locally stored data remotely.
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s features, permissions, or data practices change. The updated policy will include a new effective date and will be made available through the Play Store listing and/or within the App.
